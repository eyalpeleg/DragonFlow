Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · Applies to the DragonFlow Android app (com.plgsw.dragonflow) and this website.

The short version. DragonFlow has no server and no user accounts. Everything you create stays in the app's private storage on your own device. If — and only if — you switch on Google Drive backup, a copy is written to a private folder in your own Drive that no one else can read. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

1. Who is responsible

DragonFlow is a personal, open-source project published by plgsw. The complete source code is available at github.com/eyalpeleg/DragonFlow under the Apache-2.0 licence, so every claim on this page can be verified against the code.

2. What the app stores

All of the following is created by you and kept locally on your device, in the app's private storage area. It is not transmitted anywhere by the app on its own.

DataWhat it isWhere it lives
Tasks Titles, descriptions, priority, due dates and times, status, sub-tasks, recurrence settings and completion notes On device
Categories Names and colours you assign to group tasks On device
Preferences Theme, first day of week, default task time, notification and bubble settings, Pomodoro configuration On device
Google account email Shown in Settings so you can see which account backups go to. Only present if you sign in. On device
Backup copies A snapshot of the data above, in JSON form Your Google Drive, only if you enable backup

We do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, calendar, device identifiers, advertising ID, or any record of how you use the app.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you sign in from the Settings screen. When you do, DragonFlow uses Google Sign-In and requests a single scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — access to the application data folder in your Drive.

What that scope can and cannot do

  • Backups are written to Drive's hidden appDataFolder. This area is private to DragonFlow: it does not appear in your Drive file list, and other apps cannot read it.
  • This scope gives no access to the rest of your Drive — your documents, photos and other files are invisible to the app.
  • The backup contains your tasks, categories and preferences, and nothing else.
  • The data is transferred directly between your device and Google's servers over HTTPS. It does not pass through any server operated by us, because there isn't one.
  • Older backups are pruned automatically so only recent snapshots are kept.

Limited use disclosure

DragonFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through these APIs is used solely to back up and restore your own tasks, is never sold, never transferred to third parties, and is never used for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

Turning it off

Sign out from the Settings screen to stop backing up. You can delete the stored backups at any time by revoking DragonFlow's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also removes the app data folder, or by deleting hidden app data from Drive's settings.

4. Manual export and import

You can export your data to a JSON file and share or save it wherever you choose. Once that file leaves the app it is entirely in your hands — the destination you send it to is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not this one.

5. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy it is needed
Notifications Due-date reminders, daily task summaries and Pomodoro alerts. All notifications are generated on your device; none are pushed from a server.
Display over other apps Draws the optional floating bubble showing your urgent task count. Reads nothing from the apps underneath it.
Run at startup Restores the floating bubble after the device reboots.
Foreground service Keeps the bubble alive while the app is in the background.
Exact alarms Fires reminders at the time you set rather than whenever the system gets around to it.
Internet Used only for Google Drive backup. With backup off, the app makes no network requests.

6. Sharing text into DragonFlow

DragonFlow registers as an Android share target for text. When you share text to it from another app, that text is used to pre-fill a new task on your device. It is not sent anywhere else.

7. Third parties

The only external service the app can contact is Google — for sign-in and Drive backup, and only after you opt in. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising networks or social media trackers in the app.

8. This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party scripts or fonts. Cloudflare processes standard server request logs (such as IP address and user agent) to serve and protect the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays for as long as you keep it. Deleting a task removes it from the device. To erase everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android's app settings — and, if you used Drive backup, revoke access as described in section 3. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

10. Children

DragonFlow is a general-purpose task manager and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Past versions are visible in the repository's commit history.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy, or a correction to this page? Open an issue at github.com/eyalpeleg/DragonFlow/issues.
